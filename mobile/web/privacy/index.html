Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

ShipdeHop ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ShipdeHop P2P crowdshipping, carpooling, and marketplace platform. We are committed to maintaining community trust through absolute data transparency and robust privacy protection.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only essential information required for platform functionality and security:

  • Account Authentication: Primary sign-in uses Email Magic Links. Email addresses are collected for account authentication and service management.
  • Optional Profile Details: Phone number and profile name may be optionally provided for profile details or future verification/safety flows.
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location data is used exclusively for corridor route matching, nearby pickup searches, and active journey tracking.
  • Photos & Content: User-uploaded package inspection images and marketplace photos are processed for HopShield safety compliance and listing displays.
  • In-App Messages & Transaction Logs: Order lifecycle data and chat messages between matched counterparties are stored to facilitate active journeys and dispute prevention.
  • Identity Verification Status: Verification status metadata is maintained for verified tier badges. Raw Aadhaar numbers or government identity tokens are strictly NEVER collected or stored on server infrastructure.

3. Operational & Feature Status

  • Payment Processing: Financial payment processing is disabled in this release (`PAYMENT_PROVIDER=DISABLED`). No real monetary charges or financial data collections occur.
  • Government Identity Verification: Official UIDAI/Aadhaar production verification is currently dormant/unavailable in this release.

4. Security & Data Retention

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. When an account deletion is requested, personal profile details (email, name, phone, avatar) are deleted or anonymized, and authentication access is permanently revoked. Limited transaction logs and security audit records are retained in anonymized form where legally required for compliance and safety.

5. Account Deletion & Rights

You can request account deletion inside the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account & data or via our public Account Deletion Request Page.

6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact us at shipsterheadquarter@gmail.com.
