Account & Data Deletion Request

Options and instructions to request deletion of your ShipdeHop account and associated data.
Important Notice: Account deletion revokes authentication access and purges personal profile information. Once processed, you will no longer be able to sign in with your account.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have access to the ShipdeHop mobile app, you can initiate account deletion directly:

  1. Open the ShipdeHop mobile app.
  2. Navigate to Profile → Settings → Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm your decision in the prompt. Your personal profile details will be purged and authentication access will be permanently revoked.

Option 2: External Email Request

If you cannot access the mobile app, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send an email from your registered email address to shipsterheadquarter@gmail.com.
  • Subject line: ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST
  • Our compliance team will verify registered account ownership and process the deletion within 7 business days.

Data Retention & Anonymization Disclosures

When an account deletion is executed:

  • Personal Identifiers: Your email address, profile name, phone number, avatar URL, and active listings are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.
  • Authentication Identity: Your sign-in identity is deleted or permanently deactivated in our authentication system.
  • Legally Required Audit Records: Completed transaction logs and security safety records are retained strictly in an anonymized format for legal compliance and dispute prevention as required by applicable laws.
