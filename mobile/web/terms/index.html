Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

1. Intermediary Platform Role

ShipdeHop provides a technology platform connecting peer senders, travellers, riders, buyers, and sellers. ShipdeHop operates purely as an intermediary software provider and is not a common carrier, freight forwarder, or taxi operator.

2. User Obligations & Safety Compliance

  • Sender Responsibilities: Senders must accurately declare item contents, dimensions, and weight. Senders must consent to HopShield inspection and package verification.
  • Traveller Responsibilities: Travellers agree to transport accepted parcels safely, perform physical handoff verification using single-use OTP/QR codes, and adhere to declared routes.
  • Prohibited Items: Users are strictly forbidden from shipping illegal substances, hazardous materials, explosives, weapons, counterfeit goods, or unsealed liquids. Violations result in immediate account termination and law enforcement reporting.

3. HopRide Carpooling Guidelines

HopRide facilitates genuine cost-sharing carpooling between independent drivers and passengers travelling along shared corridors. Drivers agree not to charge rates exceeding reasonable cost-sharing limits and must maintain valid driver licenses and vehicle insurance.

4. HopShop Marketplace

HopShop allows community members to list and purchase peer-to-peer goods. All marketplace transactions are governed by physical handoff verification and state checks to ensure item satisfaction before order completion.

5. Disclaimers & Dispute Resolution

ShipdeHop provides its services on an "as is" and "as available" basis. Users agree to resolve disputes through our community dispute resolution workflow. ShipdeHop reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts engaging in fraudulent or abusive behavior.
